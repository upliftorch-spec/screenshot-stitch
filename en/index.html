Frequently Asked Questions

How many screenshots can I stitch?
You can stitch up to 20 screenshots at once. For more, stitch in batches and combine the results.
Can I stitch images of different sizes?
Yes. For vertical stitching, images are centered based on the widest image. For horizontal, they are centered based on the tallest image.
Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your screenshots never leave your device.
How do I reorder screenshots?
After uploading, simply drag and drop the screenshot items to rearrange their order before stitching.
How does automatic duplicate-content removal work?
The tool compares the end of one screenshot with the beginning of the next and removes the matching repeated area. You can turn it off before arranging if you want to keep every pixel.
What output formats are available?
Choose PNG for lossless quality, JPEG for a smaller file, or PDF for a document split across standard pages.
